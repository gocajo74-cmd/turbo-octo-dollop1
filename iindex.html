Decision Lab

Compare your options when you do not know what will happen. Type your numbers and the page shows which alternative wins under Maximax, Maximin, Minimax Regret and EMV, and explains why.

How to use it
  1. Name your alternatives (rows: what you can choose) and your states of nature (columns: what might happen).
  2. Fill in the table. Enter the payoffs directly, or enter units, revenues and costs and let the page calculate the payoffs.
  3. Read the results below. Every card shows the winner and the reason.

Probability sensitivity

Optional. Use this when a probability depends on an unknown p, for example P(S1) = p and P(S2) = p/2. For each state except the last, enter its multiplier of p (1 for p, 0.5 for p/2, 2 for 2p). The last state takes the rest: 1 minus the sum of the others.
